# From Maxwell's equations to the EFIE

{.mom-status}
{bdg-secondary-line}`Planned` Outline of a future page.

Why the solver only needs a mesh of the metal surface: the fields everywhere follow from the surface
current, and the current is fixed by one condition on the metal.

**This page will cover**

- Time-harmonic Maxwell equations; surface currents on a perfect electric conductor (PEC).
- Magnetic vector potential $\mathbf A$ and electric scalar potential $\Phi$, free-space Green's
  function.
- The boundary condition: zero tangential total electric field on the PEC surface.
- The electric field integral equation (EFIE), and its two right-hand sides: a gap source
  (radiation) and an incident plane wave (scattering).
- What the model leaves out: dielectrics, substrates and ground planes are not modelled unless
  meshed as metal (free space only).
