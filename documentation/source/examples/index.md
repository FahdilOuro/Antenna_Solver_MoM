# Examples

{.mom-status}
{bdg-secondary-line}`Planned` Outline of a future page.

A gallery of the example notebooks of `solver/antenna_simulation/`, one card per antenna, each with
a figure regenerated from the current solver and a link to read the notebook online (Plotly figures
do not render on GitHub; [nbviewer](https://nbviewer.org/) shows them).

Planned groups, following the folders of `solver/antenna_simulation/`:

- radiation: dipoles, bowtie, loops, IFA, monopole, arrays, lumped elements;
- multi-port: the 4-port circularly polarised CubeSat (`Nice_Cube`);
- gap-source studies (`gap_voltage_implementation`) and frequency sweeps;
- scattering: plate, slot, bowtie, IFA;
- characteristic modes: plate, rectangular loop, CubeSat;
- optimisation: the parasitic-array study of `euraap_challenge`;
- meshing: refinement studies.
